1️⃣ HOMEPAGE AUDIT

✅ What’s Good
	•	Clear hero headline: “Join our premier sports facility…”
	•	Real facility images (builds trust).
	•	Wide activity range shown.
	•	Address & contact visible.
	•	Booking link highlighted.

❌ Issues & Improvement

1. Hero Section Not Impactful Enough
	•	Static hero image → feels flat, not premium.
	•	No emotional connection (family, kids, community not shown).
	•	CTA (“Enroll for Trial”) is visible but not standout.

Fix:
	•	Use dynamic hero: loop video of real badminton courts, pickleball action, kids training.
	•	Add two CTAs:
✔ “Book Free Trial” (Primary)
✔ “Explore Activities” (Secondary)

2. Visual Hierarchy Needs Stronger Structure
	•	Sections feel similar in weight.
	•	No strong separators between key blocks.

Fix:
	•	Use large headings, bold sections, better spacing, 2-tone backgrounds.

3. Your Strongest Selling Points Are Missing
You have huge strengths:
	•	6 BWF-certified courts
	•	20+ years academy
	•	Multi-sport hub in Baner–Aundh
	•	Family-friendly
	•	Coaching + membership + bookings

But none of these are highlighted properly.

Fix:
Create a “WHY CHOOSE US” section with large icons.

4. Activities Grid is Basic
	•	All cards look same → user doesn’t feel “premium facility”.

Fix:
	•	Use modern card design → hover animation, dark overlays, sharp typography.

5. Testimonials Not Strong Enough
	•	Low emotional impact.
	•	No photos / videos / ratings.

Fix:
	•	Add photos of real members & parents.
	•	Add Google rating badge (4.8★ etc.)
	•	Add short reels of coaching sessions.

6. Missing “Family” Positioning
Your academy’s USP = kids + adults can train together (badminton especially).
It is nowhere on homepage.

Fix:
Add a section like:
“A Sports Academy for the Entire Family”
with images of parent/kid groups.
